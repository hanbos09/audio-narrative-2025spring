Hannah's Audio Narrative
(assets listed at the bottom)
At the start of this project, I wanted to recreate what an average night after class was like for me. Originally, I just had what would be my boyfriend playing Marvel Rivals in the background while I did homework. From this, I wanted to add our cat, Poppy, meowing and purring while interrupting both of our work, as she always does. However, I found that this extremely simple vision was actually much harder for me to imagine through a soundscape.
My first draft just included playthrough of Marvel Rivals, a cat meowing, a furnace in the background, and a PS5 controller sound. I really struggled with where to go from here, just settling for what it was without expanding on the idea.
Much later on, I thought I would just have more fun with the idea, instead taking what I think is my boyfriend's perspective of me getting home. Now in my project, you can still hear his playthrough with our cat meowing, but now you hear me coming through the door and walking over to him. Then, as I approach and get closer, the world around him goes quiet. When I finally reach where he is at, I kiss him on the cheek and he immediately transports into a world of serenity and pure love. At the end, though, Poppy always has to make it about herself so it ends with a meow.
Obviously this must be exactly how he feels when I get home from school.
I found that when I just focused on making something that would make both him and myself laugh, it was much easier to let my imagination fly and have fun with it.
